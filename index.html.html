About The Symposium

Where Medicine Meets Engineering

MEDVERSE’26 is a National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Biomedical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Dr. M.G.R. Educational and Research Institute.

The symposium brings together students and young innovators to explore biomedical engineering, medical technology, creativity, problem-solving and innovation.

AURAMED – The Rhythm of Medical Innovation

AURAMED represents the continuous rhythm of innovation in healthcare, where engineering, medicine and technology converge to create meaningful solutions for the future.

NATIONAL LEVEL

Welcoming aspiring engineers and healthcare innovators across premier institutions nationwide.

TECHNICAL EVENTS

Paper presentations (Pitch Point), scientific posters (Pinnacle), case diagnosis (Case Quest), and rapid quizzes (Brain Rot).

NON-TECHNICAL

High-stakes esports gaming, photographic vision, face painting artistry, and meme-culture challenges.

BIOMEDICAL INNOVATION

Translating groundbreaking scientific thought into tangible, life-saving clinical applications.
